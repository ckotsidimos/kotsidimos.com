About

I work on complex systems of systems where speed, scale, and governance intersect. My focus is enabling flow in environments constrained by data gravity, regulation, and economic reality.

I treat organizations as collections of independent but coordinated functions, designed to be recomposed as conditions change rather than optimized for static efficiency.

Orientation

My work treats organizations as evolving systems that absorb change structurally, rather than through episodic transformation initiatives.
